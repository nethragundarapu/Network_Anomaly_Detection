Telemetry Schema Standards

  • duration: Numeric connection lifespan in seconds
  • packets: Total ingress and egress packet count
  • bytes: Total transfer payload in bytes
  • failed_connections: TCP SYN/RST handshakes or auth drops
  • unique_ports: Number of unique destination service ports
  • label: Binary truth (0 = Benign, 1 = Suspicious)
